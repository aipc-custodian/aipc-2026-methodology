# The sample

Fetch every article listed into `../Style Guide/style1/papers/`, as PDFs, and nothing else. Where there is no open-access copy, fetch the published version through the library.

- **The Philosophical Review** — Ranked first among general philosophy journals in Leiter's 2022 poll.
- **Noûs** — Ranked second in the same poll.
- **Philosophy and Phenomenological Research** — Ranked third in the same poll.
- **Mind** — Ranked fourth in the same poll.
- **The Journal of Philosophy** — Ranked fifth in the same poll.

Rankings used: I used the top five of Brian Leiter's 2022 poll of 'best general philosophy journals' (Philosophical Review, Noûs, PPR, Mind, Journal of Philosophy), which matches the top five of his earlier polls apart from small reorderings.
