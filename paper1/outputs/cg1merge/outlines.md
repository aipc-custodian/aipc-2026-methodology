# Outlines — merged

**Thesis (fixed):** Common ground is not a single phenomenon but the accidental convergence of at least two independently operating mechanisms: one tracking mutual public commitment, which licenses what can be presupposed in assertion, and another tracking mutual representation, which coordinates joint activity — conversation included — and licenses what can be insinuated (and otherwise conveyed off-record) within it.

Nothing here is ranked and nothing is scored. The outlines were written blind to each other, and the flags below each one are computed from its own dependency ledger — no model judged coherence. Read the outlines first; the divergence section at the end is only useful once you have.

## Outline merged — Common Ground as Accidental Convergence

*Establish that two classes of conversational move are licensed by different conditions — one requiring public settlement, one requiring its absence — show the two come apart again at repair, argue from the inversion to independence, then show unified accounts cannot absorb both without idle structure.*

- **Surprise.** The two mechanisms are shown to make opposite demands on the same content rather than merely different ones: presupposition needs public settlement, insinuation needs its absence, and deniability is the gap between them. The convergence is then explained rather than left brute — both answer to the same communicative inputs — which is what earns the word 'accidental' instead of asserting it.
- **Commitment.** It denies that the differentials can be absorbed as performance effects or context-sensitivity — the two must be structural. A cautious version would claim only that the phenomena are theoretically distinct while drawing on one resource; this one denies the shared resource.
- **Gap.** §7, abductive: The two differentials must be best explained by independent mechanisms rather than by one mechanism with two modes of access; this requires that no single-state account can generate a publicity asymmetry and a repair asymmetry together without structure introduced solely to produce them.
- **Arrives at.** §9
- **Load-bearing.** Presupposition-licensing is sensitive to public accessibility in a way that coordination is not: assertion fails when a presupposition lacks public standing even where the speaker correctly believes the hearer shares the relevant mental state.
