**The Thesis Forge: Methodology Report, Stage One**

*Candidate generation and adversarial ranking*

**Purpose.** The Thesis Forge is the front end of a multi-stage pipeline for producing philosophical papers. It does not write. It generates candidate theses in a specified area, subjects the strongest to adversarial objection, and returns them ranked on two independent axes so that a human can select which are worth developing. It is deliberately not a filter: it eliminates nothing, and the verdict on each thesis is reported as a field alongside it rather than used to discard it.

**Architecture.** Five stages. (1) Generation: N model instances each produce candidate theses at high temperature, blind to one another, prompted to look for a specific kind of move — an undrawn distinction, a counterexample to a shared premise, a reframing, an instability, an unnoticed commitment. (2) Appraisal: a separate instance scores each thesis for promise, comparatively and in batches, having seen no objections. (3) Clustering: near-duplicates are merged, with the highest-scoring member kept and the others recorded on it. (4) Attack: the highest-promise theses face a Killer assigned one specific line of objection, a Defender required to mount a real defence before conceding, and an Adjudicator that scores each objection for damage and rules whether it was met or merely absorbed. (5) Report: theses are ranked by promise and by residual damage — the strength of the strongest objection left unanswered.

**Method of development.** The system was built iteratively against its own output, in collaboration with an AI assistant, over roughly a dozen revisions. Every substantive change was prompted by reading transcripts rather than by reasoning in advance about what would work. Several findings were counterintuitive enough that they could not have been anticipated:

**Dialogue destroys variance where variance is the product.** The project began from a recording of two model instances conversing freely. Such loops converge: identical agents mirror and amplify each other. Generation therefore uses isolated parallel samples with no conversation at all. Dialogue is reserved for applying pressure to a fixed object.

**Adversarial survival selects against ambition.** In early runs the surviving theses were consistently the least promising ones. Modest claims present small targets; structural claims about a whole debate are refuted by a single counterexample. Ranking by survival alone therefore inverts the ordering one wants. The two axes are reported separately for this reason, and a distinct category collects high-promise theses that failed on one locatable flaw, for which the adjudicator supplies the narrower surviving version.

**Any escape the design permits will be taken.** Left to choose its objection, the Killer used the same one every time — equivocation can be manufactured against any compressed thesis. Given a licence to decline inapplicable objections, it declined ten of twelve. Given a dedicated check on whether a thesis's named target actually holds the view ascribed to it, it found misattribution in nineteen of twenty-one. Each fix produced the same failure in a new location. The current design constrains by assignment and rotation rather than by instruction, and treats an unattacked thesis as untested rather than as surviving.

**Human involvement.** Two gates are planned: selection among ranked theses, and selection among finished drafts. Both are selections from machine-generated options rather than contributions of content. A third role emerged unplanned and proved more consequential. On a topic within the author's specialism, the highest-ranked thesis was found to rest on a factual error about a named theorist's position — an error invisible to every component of the system, since the objections are generated from model recall with no access to text. The same run's clustering stage had buried four theses that got the point right. Neither failure was detectable on topics outside the author's competence, where the output looked equally plausible.

**Known limitations.** Claims about the existing literature, including every judgment that a thesis is novel, are produced from recall without retrieval and should be treated as hypotheses. The residual-damage score cannot distinguish a thesis that withstood heavy objection from one that attracted only weak objection; the strongest objection mounted is therefore reported alongside it. Yield is highly variable — across three runs on one question the system produced four, one, and zero theses meeting both criteria.
