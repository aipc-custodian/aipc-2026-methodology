**Retrieval Check and Outline Deviser: Methodology Report, Stage Two**

Structural devising, and the check the front end lacked

**Purpose.** Stage two takes one thesis selected from the Thesis Forge's gate report and produces the argument structure of a paper. It comprises two components. The Outline Deviser writes several independent outlines of the paper the thesis would need, and audits each for internal coherence. The Retrieval Check runs twice — once on the shortlisted theses before any outlining, and once on the finished outline — and is the pipeline's only contact with published text. Neither component ranks or eliminates anything. As in stage one, verdicts are fields reported alongside the object rather than filters applied to it.

**Architecture: the Outline Deviser.** N outlines are generated from the surviving restatement of the thesis, at high temperature, blind to one another, with no adversarial stage and no ranking. Six outlines do not exceed one reader's attention, and adjudication would interpose a worse reader between the reader and the documents. Seven requirements are imposed as conditions on production rather than as criteria applied afterwards: every section states the claim it establishes and what would refute it, with the refutation condition naming a position someone actually holds; every section states what it takes as given and from where; no section may exist that nothing downstream needs; the argument must reach the thesis, by deduction, strong induction or abduction indifferently; the outline must locate its own widest inferential gap; the most-depended-upon claim must be defended in proportion; and an introduction and conclusion are required but exempted from all of the above. Section count is set by the argument. Each outline then passes a wholly mechanical audit, which computes coherence from the dependency ledger rather than asking a model to judge it, and a divergence pass reports which structural moves every outline makes and which they split on.

**Architecture: the Retrieval Check.** The novelty check searches the live web through a server-side search tool, with no corpus assembled in advance, and is required to search adjacent empirical literatures rather than philosophy alone; a search that never left philosophy is reported as a failed search rather than a clean result. It then selects entries from the Stanford Encyclopedia's own contents index — so that a fabricated entry is not a possible output — and reads them in full, on the reasoning that an encyclopedia entry is written to name the positions in a debate, which makes its silence informative in a way an empty web search is not. Findings are classified as anticipating, contradicting, supporting or merely adjacent to the thesis, since those call for different responses, and the check is required to state what it searched for and failed to find. The attribution check verifies that each cited work exists, mechanically, against a bibliographic index with no model involved, and then asks of each named position whether the person holds the view ascribed to them.

**Method of development.** As in stage one, every substantive change followed from reading output rather than from reasoning in advance, and several findings could not have been anticipated.

> *The adversarial architecture does not transfer.* A thesis is a claim that can be attacked; an outline is a structure, against which the stage-one objections are category mistakes. The architecture that survives is isolation and variance; the ranking machinery does not, because it existed to manage two hundred objects and there are now six.
> 
> *Coherence is better computed than judged.* Each section reproduces verbatim the claim it takes from an earlier section. Arguments drift upward in paraphrase — a section establishes something modest and a later one quotes it back slightly stronger — and requiring the same characters makes the drift mechanically detectable rather than a matter of interpretation. It caught a real instance on the second run, where a conceptual gap was quoted back as an explanatory gap, the substitution falling on precisely the term the earlier section had been establishing.
> 
> *Any parameter the design states becomes a target.* Instructed to write between five and nine sections, the system wrote seven, ten times in twelve, across theses in six unrelated areas of philosophy. Seven is the midpoint of the stated range. Removing the range and requiring the count to follow the argument's dependencies produced counts from four to eight. This is the stage-one finding that any permitted escape will be taken, arriving through a number rather than through a licence.
> 
> *Isolation prevents mirroring but not convergence.* Six outlines written blind to one another nevertheless produced a single argument with its sections permuted. The cause was that each had been given the generating stage's sketch of how the argument might go, which already contained most of it. Isolation protects against agents converging on each other; it does not protect against agents converging on a prior they were all handed.
> 
> *The binding constraint was the limitation stage one had recorded and set aside.* The stage-one report notes that all claims about existing literature are produced from recall without retrieval and should be treated as hypotheses. That limitation turned out to be the one that mattered. A thesis ranked at maximum promise with no unanswered objection lost its empirical claim to a psychological literature that had established it, and its structural move to a paper published in 2015 — both found by ordinary search within minutes. No amount of adversarial pressure detects priority, because the objection stage is generated from the same recall as the thesis.

**Human involvement.** The stage-one report described the human gates as selections from machine-generated options rather than contributions of content, and recorded a third role that emerged unplanned: the author, working within his specialism, catching an error about a named theorist that no component could catch. Stage two formalises that role as a review conversation, and the record of one complete run shows it doing two different things. Most interventions were adjudications of flags the system raised: a thin-load-bearer warning prompted the merging of two outlines, three separate attribution verdicts prompted three replacements of named positions, and three findings of prior publication discarded three theses. These are selections in the stage-one sense. Four further changes were not. The author observed that the outline gave one of its two mechanisms no conversational role and would therefore read as trivial; promoted an aside about content moving between mechanisms into a section, on the ground that it was stronger evidence than anything else in the paper; broadened that mechanism's remit beyond the single case the outline had used to isolate it; and rewrote the thesis accordingly. Those are contributions of content, and the claim that the human role is purely selective does not survive them.

**Known limitations.** Retrieval establishes that something has been argued; it cannot establish that nothing has. Absence of a result is weak evidence about a largely paywalled literature, and the reports are required to say what was searched for and not found so that a clean result remains readable. The check that mattered most in practice — establishing what a 2015 paper actually argued, as against what its abstract suggested — required the full text, which required institutional access the system does not have. Of seven named positions in a hand-written outline, three did not survive attribution: one ascribed to a theorist a view he does not hold, one described two philosophers as sharing an assumption while one of them argues against the other, and one could not be confirmed at all. Only the existence check is mechanical; the verdicts on whether a view is held are themselves model judgments, and the URLs attached to findings are taken from whichever search result carried the quotation and may not point at the work cited. Finally, the Deviser produces candidate structures rather than a structure: the outline that went forward was assembled by hand from two of the six, and would not have existed otherwise.
